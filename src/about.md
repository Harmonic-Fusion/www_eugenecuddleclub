Most adults don't get much non-sexual touch, and there aren't many places to get it. Cuddle Club is one. We run structured evenings where touch is taught and practiced rather than assumed: asking clearly, hearing a real yes, saying no without cost, and staying present in contact that lasts. Everything is sober, platonic, and held by facilitators who stay in the room.

## Our values

### 1. Consent is a practice, not a policy.

We don't announce rules and move on. We teach asking and declining, and we build repetitions into the evening.

*In practice:* every event includes taught, practiced consent work before open time.

### 2. A no should cost nothing.

Consent is only real if refusing is socially free. We watch for the pressure nobody says out loud: the pause, the polite yes, the person who doesn't want to seem rude.

*In practice:* facilitators notice ambiguous yeses and step in without making anyone the villain, and we create clear opt-out options.

### 3. Platonic by design.

Our containers are non-sexual. That's not a judgment of erotic space. It's a promise about what this one is, and the promise is what makes it possible to relax.

*In practice:* we define this by behavior in writing, so nobody has to guess where the line is. See the [code of conduct](/code-of-conduct/).

### 4. We teach, we don't just host.

Structure and facilitation are core. Every event has something taught in it.

*In practice:* if an event has no taught practice, it isn't a Cuddle Club event.

### 5. The container is staffed.

Safety comes from people, not from a code of conduct on a webpage.

*In practice:* attendance is capped to what our facilitator count can actually hold. We turn people away before we run thin.

## Our events

We run two formats. Both are sober, platonic, facilitated, and consent-based, and both are hosted at {{ site.venue }}. The difference is what the evening asks of you.

### Snuggle Dance

A workshop in touch, presence, and connection, built around movement.

The taught practice at its center is **Micro Fusion**, a partner dance style built around slow, responsive, consent-explicit connection. We start open and move closer, and each progression is an opportunity to step back if you need to. Small movements, big awareness. You'll practice moving between disconnected, open position, closed position, and close embrace, both non-verbally and with words.

Alongside the dance, there's a facilitated consent-based touch game to help everyone practice sharing and exchanging touch as the evening transitions toward snuggles.

The rest of the evening is open time to put it into practice: social dancing, cuddle space indoors, and a fire pit outside, with facilitators circulating throughout both spaces.

### Snuggle Puddle

A slower evening, focused on settling into touch and resting together.

There's no partner dance to learn here. Snuggle Puddle is about sustained, unhurried contact: getting comfortable, staying a while, and letting your nervous system actually downshift. Most of the evening happens on cushions, mats, and comfortable seating rather than on your feet.

The taught practice is consent itself. We work through structured touch exercises where you practice asking for exactly the touch you want, hearing a no without it costing anything, and making a counter-offer instead of just going along with something. These are short, repeatable, and low-stakes by design, so the skills are in your body before open cuddle time begins.

From there it's open time in the puddle, with facilitators present throughout. There are regular points in the evening where everyone is invited to get up, stretch, move around, and reconnect, so nobody gets stuck on the periphery and nobody feels locked into where they landed.
