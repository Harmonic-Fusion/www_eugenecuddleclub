## Touch

- All touch includes consent from all people involved.
- Non-verbal cues are important for consent and we use words when we need to clarify.
- Anyone can stop or change anything at any time, without a reason and without apologizing.
- If you're not sure, stop and ask.

## Non-sexual container

- No sexual touch. Clothes stay on. No contact with genitals, breasts, or buttocks. No grinding, no kissing.
- Arousal happens and it isn't shameful. Shift position or take a break. Don't make it someone else's problem and don't comment on someone else's body.
- No propositioning anyone during the event. If you want to ask someone out, do it another day, off site.

## The space

- Sober event. No alcohol or other substances before or during, including vaping and smoking anywhere on the property.
- No photos or video.
- Don't share the address.
- Don't share who you saw here.

## Children

- Children stay in the childcare space. They do not enter the dance floor, cuddle space, or fire pit.
- Childcare is staffed by an experienced adult at all times.
- If you brought a child, you are reachable at all times.

## If something goes wrong

- Tell any facilitator. You do not need proof, and you do not need to be sure.
- You will not be asked to confront anyone or to explain yourself in front of others.
- We will act on it, including asking someone to leave if necessary.

## Removal

We can ask anyone to leave at any time, for any reason, without debate at the door. Tickets are refunded. Repeated or serious violations mean not being invited back.
