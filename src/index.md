<h2 id="about">A different kind of touch practice</h2>

Most adults don't get much non-sexual touch, and there aren't many places to get it. Cuddle Club is one. We run structured evenings where touch is taught and practiced rather than assumed: asking clearly, hearing a real yes, saying no without cost, and staying present in contact that lasts. Everything is sober, platonic, and held by facilitators who stay in the room.

We hope this helps you build the confidence and community to support the need of touch.

<ul class="facts">
  <li><strong>Sober</strong> No alcohol or other substances, including vaping and smoking, anywhere on the property.</li>
  <li><strong>Platonic by design</strong> Clothes stay on. The container is non-sexual, and that promise is what makes it possible to relax.</li>
  <li><strong>Facilitated</strong> Multiple facilitators hold the space for the full evening, not just the opening.</li>
  <li><strong>A workshop, not a party</strong> Expect structure and a real practice to leave with.</li>
</ul>

## What to expect

- Facilitators present and holding the space for the full evening.
- A discussion at the start to set intentions and shared norms.
- Something taught and practiced hands-on, not just talked about.
- Open time afterward to put it into practice.


<div class="callout callout--pink">

**All touch here is invited, not assumed.** Every activity is consent based. You are in choice the whole time, and there is always an opt-out space you can step into and rejoin whenever you like.

</div>
