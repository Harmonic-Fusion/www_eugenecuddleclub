## A different kind of touch practice

Our events are facilitated evenings for real, sustained, consent-forward touch, held in a safe container, without sexual energy attached to it.

This isn't a substitute for the connection you already have on the dance floor. Where partner and ecstatic dance give us touch that's brief and held inside the shape of a song, our evenings are about staying present with touch that lasts: asking clearly, listening for a real yes, and settling into contact without a beat telling you when to let go.

<ul class="facts">
  <li><strong>Sober</strong> No alcohol or other substances, including vaping and smoking, anywhere on the property.</li>
  <li><strong>Platonic by design</strong> Clothes stay on. The container is non-sexual, and that promise is what makes it possible to relax.</li>
  <li><strong>Facilitated</strong> Multiple facilitators hold the space for the full evening, not just the opening.</li>
  <li><strong>A workshop, not a party</strong> Expect structure and a real practice to leave with.</li>
</ul>

## Two kinds of evening

**Snuggle Dance** is built around movement. We teach Micro Fusion, a slow, responsive, consent-explicit partner dance, then open into social dancing, cuddle space, and a fire pit outside.

**Snuggle Puddle** is slower and closer to the ground. There's no dance to learn: we practice consent and touch directly through structured exercises, then spend the evening settling into sustained contact on cushions and mats.

[Read more about both](/about/)

## What to expect

- Multiple facilitators present and holding the space for the full evening.
- A discussion at the start to set intentions and shared norms.
- Something taught and practiced hands-on, not just talked about.
- Open time afterward to put it into practice, with facilitators circulating.
- Regular invitations to get up, move around, and reconnect, so nobody gets stuck on the periphery.

<div class="callout">

**All touch here is invited, not assumed.** Every activity is consent based, and no is always a complete sentence. You are in choice the whole time, and there is always an opt-out space you can step into and rejoin whenever you like.

</div>
